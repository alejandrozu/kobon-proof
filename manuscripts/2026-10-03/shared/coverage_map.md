# Coverage of the March–October Kobon work

This companion is part of both 3 October 2026 manuscript editions. The old
60-page and 14-page PDFs remain preserved under `paper/`. Here “complete
coverage” means every distinct mathematical contribution, historical claim,
finite-certificate identity and research direction is retained with its
actual evidence level. It does not mean every old statement has become a
Lean theorem or every earlier numerical value was a new record.

The [proof map](proof_map.md) preserves all 89 earlier claim IDs and all 138
coordinate identities, of which 104 have simple witnesses. Its source links
are immutable. All new results below refer to the successful active proof
release at `2d69a71e32d3eaa59399b0e5bc61720ec3b00ac8`.

## Historical results and finite checkpoints

The original March claim and incomplete archived source are retained as
history, not an active proof. The three inequalities 28:238, 30:275, 34:357
have active exact witnesses; previous literature overlaps are acknowledged.
The eleven retained phase/cap gains at orders 39, 47, 48, 51, 53, 54, 55, 59, 60, 99, 195
remain represented by formulas, the selected table and the complete catalog.
The simple 44:608 certificate now combines with a fully formalized simple
upper theorem; its substitution is a corollary, not a separate named wrapper.

The compatible 11:32 and 21:132 inputs need not beat every known construction
at those orders. Finite 81/82/161/162 checkpoints remain certified. The larger
321/322/641/642 **existence bounds** now follow from the infinite Lean family;
this does not retroactively certify each earlier saved coordinate file. In
particular the older 642-line file's unfinished second counter remains
unfinished. Finite identity count 138 has not been inflated by those new
existence theorems.
