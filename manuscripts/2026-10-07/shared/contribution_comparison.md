## Formal upgrades of inherited results

The 33-, 37- and 49-line compatible seeds now feed complete infinite
odd/even families and simple optimality proofs. Their numerical orbits are
the known Forge–Ramírez Alfonsín, Parpalak–Utkin/Blanc and BBL families.
Closing exact seed checks and recursive interfaces is a formal contribution;
it does not establish a new numerical family or transfer simple optimality
to arrangements with multiple intersections.

The inherited classical simple upper bounds and the former weighted,
two-core and three-core estimates now have actual geometric Lean endpoints.
Known finite inputs retain individual credits. The simple 44:608 optimality
statement is still the immediate substitution of its finite lower certificate
into the simple upper theorem, with no separately named wrapper asserted.

Actual tangent enclosures, sound memoized Boolean reflection and sparse
integer boxes make finite checks tractable. Their soundness uses standard
axioms; executed seed checks additionally use explicitly inventoried native
evaluation roots. Build and theorem counts describe implementation coverage,
not the number of original mathematical discoveries.

## Evidence that remains outside a complete Lean theorem

The concrete 41-line normalized-cell dual is an exact external calculation
for that input and parameter regime. The complete supplied 21-line affine
obstruction corpus also remains external computation, with classification
completeness attributed to the original source.

The remaining zero-component equality decomposition is ordinary mathematical
analysis. The R22→N13 and R22→R04 boundary couplings remain open. N13 being
unable to maximize an affine functional does not exclude its adjacency to
a maximizing R22 core. The global M22 theorem is complete, but there is no
componentwise M22 extension in the completed portfolio.

Bounded cap-cone, corridor, projective-chart, deletion, mutation and
straightening screens preserve unsuccessful cases and exact diagnostic
counterexamples. Their failure does not establish unrestricted infeasibility,
nonstretchability, sharpness, or the absence of a stronger construction.
