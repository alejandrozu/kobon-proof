The repository's older evidence records remain available. An explicit OEIS
table omission does not establish novelty, and numerical improvements over
the repository's saved envelope do not establish improvements over every
published construction. No numerical first-priority claim is made for the
known 33/37/49 orbits or Poola's 1190 seed. The 29-pair uniform refit and its
one-unit even improvement are concrete advances over the preserved 28-pair
family. A bounded primary-source review located no 60-dyadic 1190/4790
straight-line orbit in the inspected BBL, Parpalak–Utkin and Savchuk papers;
this is a comparison observation, not an exhaustive novelty certificate.
No new unrestricted numerical upper formula is claimed.

The distinctions that most affect correctness are:

1. **All n versus all maxima.** H is a proven construction-based lower bound
   at every natural order. It does not match every best-known individual value.
2. **Compatible doubling versus quantitative successor.** The fixed seed
   invariants close BBL iteration. A separate now-completed theorem supplies
   indefinite one-line extension from every supplied simple seed, with a
   precise deficit-dependent gain; it does not prove the old full half-order
   gain from arbitrary seeds or permit nonsimple input without a new argument.
3. **Simple versus unrestricted.** Both simple upper polynomials are formal.
   Actual nonsimple structural estimates now have complete fan extraction;
   they retain their local correction counts and arrangement-class hypotheses.
4. **Exact external versus Lean.** The 236-class obstruction coverage and full
   concrete 41-line sign-cell dual are exact external computations. The 49-line
   seed and its odd/even recursion are now complete in Lean, with native finite
   roots disclosed separately. Remaining equality analysis is ordinary proof
   analysis, not a new Lean theorem.
5. **Existence versus fixed coordinates.** A family theorem can prove a large
   numerical lower bound while a separate saved coordinate file's independent
   verification remains incomplete.
