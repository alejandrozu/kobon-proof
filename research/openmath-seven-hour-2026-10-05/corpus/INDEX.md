# Corpus branch evidence and proof map

`VERIFICATION.json` records the completed Lean source hashes, final successful
build logs, selected declaration axioms, and exact artifact identities. Its
scope excludes failed diagnostic builds and uncompleted drafts. Every JSON
also states its own hypotheses; a finite or numerical screen does not prove
a general theorem or LP infeasibility.

## Sources and priority

- `source-inventory.json` and `TECHNIQUES_AND_OBSTRUCTIONS.md`: public competition
  source pins, proof status, scalable techniques, and scoped failures.
- `SUCCESSOR_ATTRIBUTION.md`: the older perfect odd-to-even exterior
  construction versus the broader defect-two and quantitative extensions.
- `INFINITE_FAMILY_PRIORITY_CHECK.md`: primary-source checks for the new
  uniform 60-dyadic construction, including the distinction between straight
  and pseudoline arrangements.
- `PERFECT_CORE_ATTRIBUTION.md`: the previously stated perfect-implies-simple
  conclusion and the false auxiliary shared-side count in its earlier proof.

## Retained experiments

`DESINGULARIZATION_AUDIT.md` pins the exact 2026 preprint theorem and explains
the four-line and six-line witnesses without making a broader invalidity
claim. The translation certifier grew into an independent general tool.

The exact shared-edge counterexamples, radial ledgers, marked-component
screens, and explicit allowed antipodal-to-marked/one-cap adjacencies are
retained here. `two-antipodal-recipient-stress.json` is a bounded cap-completion
screen; `two-antipodal-star-candidates.json` keeps exact lines and profiles
for 36 multiple-star arrangements to support future targeted geometry.

`corridor-subarrangements/README.md` gives the original author's attribution,
source commit, and license for the finite subset data. The failed fixed-axis
nesting and insertion routes are evidence about those supplied types, not
proofs that a general corridor recurrence is impossible.

The separate `seed_search/` directory contains the bounded LP, chamber,
projective chart, elliptic template, harmonic, and retained-backbone searches.
Their negative outcomes are scoped in each report. The parent research
branch assembles successful constructions and aggregate upper bounds; this
corpus proof map does not duplicate its final theorem catalogue.
