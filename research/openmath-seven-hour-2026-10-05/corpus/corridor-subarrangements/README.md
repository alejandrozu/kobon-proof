# Attribution and scope of corridor subset data

The retained eight-line subsets are derived from Andrea Maiorana's public
14-line arrangements, distributed under CC BY 4.0 in
[rufio72/kobon_triangles_k14](https://github.com/rufio72/kobon_triangles_k14),
at commit `e47c7cfd9661e54d29befb83118bf83d5f804028`.
Andrea Maiorana retains attribution for those source constructions.

The 2026-10-05 research computation selected subsets, converted the published
coordinates to exact rational line coefficients, and independently counted
bounded triangular cells. Each JSON records its own source arrangement and
selected labels. The computation found 99 subsets with eight lines and 15
triangles, covering 20 affine types under the fixed ordering convention.
This finite containment evidence does not assert a general doubling rule,
an infinite family, or an exhaustive negative result at larger orders.

The source coordinates are changed only as explicitly recorded by each
artifact's conversion, subset selection, or fitted projective chart.
The license is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
