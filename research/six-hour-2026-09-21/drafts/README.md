# Proof drafts outside the verified library

`TamuraSeed33.lean` is a generated candidate real parameter seed proof. Its
rational-box prechecks passed, but two resource-intensive Lean builds were
stopped before completion. It is **not a verified theorem in this release**.
The finite 33 and 34 line witnesses are checked separately. The generator
`scripts/generate_tamura_seed33.py` writes this draft location deliberately.

The active `Kobon/` library and its axiom audit are the authority for completed
formal results. Preserving a draft here does not assert that Lean accepts it.
